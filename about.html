Our Tradition & Hygiene

The Annapurna Story

Rooted in the eternal Telugu ethos of అతిథి దేవో భవ (Atithi Devo Bhava) — honoring sacred culinary traditions with uncompromising food safety and master craftsmanship across Andhra Pradesh and Hyderabad.

Our Journey

Serving Authentic Andhra Flavors for Over 18 Years

At అన్నపూర్ణ (Annapurna Caterers), every feast is treated as a sacred ritual of hospitality. Born from an uncompromising passion for time-honored Telugu cooking, we bring together the regional culinary legacies of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana: the celebrated river-coastal curries of Godavari and Nellore (Chepala Pulusu, Royyala Vepudu), the royal slow-cooked dum recipes of Nizami Hyderabad, and the bold, rustic spices of Guntur and Rayalaseema (Gongura Mutton, Natu Kodi Pulusu, Ragi Sankati).

Whether catering an intimate 50-person family Gruhapravesam or a grand 2,500-guest wedding reception with ceremonial Arati Aaku (banana leaf) seating, our master chefs preserve age-old recipes. We grind whole spices fresh for each event, avoid all artificial additives, and cook exclusively in churned pure cow ghee and cold-pressed oils.

The Quality Pledge

Our Uncompromising Standards

Every banquet prepared by Annapurna Caterers is backed by rigorous dietary purity, culinary sanctity, and audited food safety protocols.

Sanctity & Tradition

100% Strictly Separate Kitchens

Complete physical segregation for pure vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus. We maintain distinct cooking premises, separate vessels, dedicated chefs, and isolated transport containers so our vegetarian clients enjoy total peace of mind for sacred poojas and weddings.

  • Distinct cookware and brass cauldrons
  • Separate cold storage & prep stations
  • Dedicated Satvik teams for Vratams & Poojas
Authentic Ingredients

Pure Cow Ghee & Cold-Pressed Oils

We strictly refuse shortcuts. All traditional sweets (Bobbatlu, Pootharekulu, Kakinada Kaja), flavored rices, and podis are prepared using genuine churned cow ghee. We use wood-pressed sesame and groundnut oils with absolutely zero synthetic food colorings, essence drops, or MSG.

  • 100% pure churned desi cow ghee
  • Hand-ground Guntur chillies & spices
  • Zero artificial food coloring or enhancers
Audited Food Safety

FSSAI Certified & ISO 9001:2015

Operating under licensed central kitchen facilities (FSSAI Lic: 13622036000123). Every culinary staff member undergoes mandatory health screenings, hygiene certifications, and daily sanitization procedures following strict ISO food safety management systems.

  • Commercial thermal-insulated food transport
  • Continuous hot-holding above 65°C
  • Periodic water and ingredient lab testing
Milestones of Excellence

Our 18-Year Culinary Journey

From our founding as an authentic family kitchen to catering high-profile weddings across Andhra Pradesh and Hyderabad.

2006 — Foundation

Humble Beginnings in Andhra

Founded with a small brigade of seasoned traditional Telugu cooks, specializing in traditional Arati Aaku (banana leaf) Vindu bhojanam and festival feasts.

2010 — First 100 Events

Building Trust in the Telugu Heartland

Crossed our milestone 100th auspicious celebration across Vijayawada and Guntur. Word-of-mouth praise established our signature Gongura Mutton and Pootharekulu recipes.

2014 — Dual Kitchens & FSSAI

100% Segregated Infrastructure

Formally commissioned strictly independent vegetarian and non-vegetarian commercial production hubs, securing FSSAI certification and setting new regional hygiene benchmarks.

2018 — 500+ Milestone

Grand Weddings & Live Counters

Expanded service capability to manage 2,000+ guest wedding banquets, introducing live counters for hot Pesarattu Upma, Jalebi, Mirchi Bajji, and Dum Biryani.

2022 — Multi-City Expansion

Serving Hyderabad & AP Major Hubs

Expanded catering logistics with insulated fleet vans covering Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Vizag, Rajahmundry, Tirupati, and Guntur with seamless on-time service.

Present — 1,200+ Events

The Gold Standard in Andhra Hospitality

Over 1,200 satisfied families, ISO 9001:2015 process compliance, and recognition as the first choice for authentic Telugu wedding feasts and corporate gatherings.
